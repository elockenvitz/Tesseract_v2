## Why this document is separate from the application terms

These terms govern **the public website** — reading the marketing pages and
asking for a demo. They are not the agreement under which anyone uses the
software.

Use of the Tesseract application is governed by
[`application-terms-draft.md`](./application-terms-draft.md), and a paying
institutional customer is governed by a signed Master Services Agreement
(`../contracts/saas-msa-draft.md`). Where those conflict with this document for
their subject matter, they win. §12 states the order.

Keeping them separate matters for one practical reason. A visitor who reads a
web page has not entered into a software licence, has not accepted a liability
cap for a product they have never used, and has not agreed to anything about
investment data. Presenting a single combined document implies they have, and
courts do not generally honour that implication.

not thereby bound to a licence, a dispute-resolution clause, or an arbitration
agreement. Where we need a user's agreement to something that matters, we ask
for it affirmatively, in the application, and we keep a record of it.
